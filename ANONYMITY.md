# Note on this copy

This is an anonymised copy of the replication package, prepared for double-blind
review. Relative to the archival version it omits the author name, the venue, the
deposit metadata file, and the version-control history.

Nothing in the research content has been altered. All record identifiers, screening
decisions, quality scores, extraction codes and database exports are unchanged, so
every count in the paper can still be reproduced from these files.

Author names appearing inside the data are the authors of the **reviewed studies**,
not of this review. They are part of the bibliographic record and are required for
the selection to be auditable.
