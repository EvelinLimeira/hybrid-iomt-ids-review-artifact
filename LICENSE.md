## Bibliographic metadata in `search/exports/`

These files are search results as returned by IEEE Xplore, Scopus, SpringerLink, the ACM
Digital Library and ScienceDirect. They are reproduced unmodified except for the removal
of every `abstract` field from the BibTeX files (the SpringerLink CSV never carried one)
and a rename to a consistent file-naming scheme, and remain subject to each provider's
terms of use. Original download names are listed in `search/queries.md`. They are
included so the search can be audited, not as an independent redistribution of those
databases.

Abstract text and the full texts of the included studies are not redistributed. Every
record carries a DOI or URL so the originals can be retrieved from the publisher.
